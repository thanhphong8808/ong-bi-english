R Two friends
> Hoa and Mai are best friends. They have a lot in common. They both love drawing and reading. Last week, they fell out. Mai borrowed Hoa's new pens and lost one. Hoa was upset and said something mean. Mai felt sad, and the two girls didn't talk for two days. Then Mai decided to apologise. She bought Hoa a new pen with her pocket money. Hoa said sorry too, because she was rude. They forgave each other and made up. Now they know that good friends are honest and kind, and they say sorry when they make mistakes.
