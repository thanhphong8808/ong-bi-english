R City or countryside?
> Some people prefer to live in the city, and others prefer the countryside. Both places have advantages and disadvantages. In the city, there are many shops, cinemas and hospitals. The air is fresh, and children can play outdoors safely. Although life there is calm, it can be boring for some young people. In my opinion, the countryside is better for children. What do you think?

> Kid: Do you know the story of the cunning fox and the crow? | Cậu có biết chuyện con cáo xảo quyệt và con quạ không?

R The legend of the watermelon
> Long ago, King Hung had an adopted son called An Tiem. An Tiem was hard-working and honest. One day, he said that his success came from his own hard work, not from the king. The king was angry, and he sent An Tiem and his wife to a lonely island. Life on the island was very difficult. One day, a bird dropped some black seeds on the sand. An Tiem planted them, and they grew into big green fruit with sweet red insides. It was the first watermelon! An Tiem and his wife sold the watermelons to passing ships. When the king heard the story, he invited An Tiem back home. The moral of the story is that hard work brings success.
